FAQ

Common questions.

What does an engagement cost?

Every project is quoted at a fixed fee after the free call, based on scope. Small projects such as a single deck or an expense audit are quoted lower than multi-month export programs.

Do you guarantee distributor deals or savings?

No. We guarantee the work: qualified targets, professional outreach, a rigorous cost review and clear deliverables. Results depend on your product, price and market.

Do you handle legal, tax or regulatory approvals?

No. We flag requirements and work alongside your accountant, lawyer, customs broker or compliance specialist. We do not give legal, tax or financial advice.

Which industries do you work with?

Mainly food and beverage, consumer products and service businesses. Cost optimisation, tracking and presentation work suits most small and mid-sized businesses.

Can you work remotely?

Yes. All work can be delivered online, for clients in any time zone.
