FAQ

Before you ask.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is a fixed fee, quoted after a free consultation. You approve scope and price before any work starts.

Do we need a gluten-free claim to work with you?

No. Gluten-free is a strength where it can be claimed, but we help any food and beverage producer that wants to sell abroad.

Do you certify products?

No. We are not a certification body or a laboratory. We show you what each market and buyer expects, plan the tests and paperwork, and prepare the documents. Certificates and lab reports come from accredited bodies.

Do you guarantee sales?

No honest consultant can. We commit to clear deliverables and deadlines. Results depend on your product, price and market.

Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. We work with producers worldwide, in English and Italian. We do not give legal, tax or financial advice and work alongside your own advisers where needed.
