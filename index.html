FAQ

Before you ask.

How much does it cost?

Every engagement is quoted after a free consultation, as a fixed fee you approve before work starts.

Which businesses do you work with?

Small and mid-sized businesses, especially food and beverage, consumer products and B2B services.

Do you guarantee savings or sales?

No honest consultant can. We commit to clear deliverables, quality and deadlines. Results depend on your product, price and market.

Do you give legal, tax or financial advice?

No. We work alongside your accountant, lawyer or other licensed advisers where it is needed.

Can export clients claim the cost?

Possibly. Consultant research and promotion can be an eligible expense under Austrade's Export Market Development Grants. EMDG is matched funding with turnover and minimum spend conditions (at least A$20,000 of your own spend on eligible activities), so it is not a refund and not guaranteed. Check eligibility with Austrade.

Can you work with us remotely?

Yes. We are based in Sydney and work with clients in any time zone.
